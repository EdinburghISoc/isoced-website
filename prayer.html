Where to pray

Multi-faith & prayer spaces on campus

You’re never far from a place to pray around the University of Edinburgh — here’s every dedicated space we know of, campus by campus.

Edinburgh Central Mosque

50 Potterrow, EH8 9BT
Our main masjid, right beside central campus and a short walk from the library — daily prayers & Jumu’ah.

Get directions →

Central Campus

The Sanctuary, Chaplaincy Centre
1 Bristo Square, EH8 9AL (ground floor, back of the auditorium)

Open Mon–Fri, 9:30am–9pm. Kitchen & study spaces too.

Get directions →

Kings Campus

Mary Brück Building, 3rd floor
Colin Maclaurin Road, EH9 3DW

Sisters’ & brothers’ prayer rooms, two common rooms and a kitchen. Open Mon–Fri, 8:30am–5pm; for out-of-hours access email the chaplaincy (allow up to 72 hours).

Get directions →

Moray House

Paterson’s Land
Holyrood Road, EH8 8AQ

Muslim prayer room via the main entrance facing Holyrood Road — stairs to the left, top floor, behind the lift. Open during building hours.

Get directions →

Lauriston Building

Chalmers Centre, room 4027
Lauriston Place, EH3 9EN (1st floor, beside Medical Photography)

A quiet space for prayer, reflection, and nursing mothers. Open during building hours.

Get directions →

Western General

Outpatients Building
Crewe Road South, EH4 2XU (ground floor)

Open 24/7 — handy for anyone on placement or visiting the hospital.

Get directions →

BioQuarter

Royal Infirmary of Edinburgh
EH16 4SB (ground floor, south corridor, next to Spiritual & Palliative Care)

Open Mon–Fri, 6:30am–7:30pm (closed during Friday Jumu’ah).

Get directions →

Easter Bush

Charnock Bradley Building, Roslin Innovation Centre
Bush Farm Road, Roslin EH25 9RG (ground floor)

Multi-cultural room with prayer times in the lobby. Open during building hours (drop-in).

Get directions →

Halal food near campus

Edinburgh is packed with halal options, including venues that proudly sponsor Edinburgh ISoc. (Always confirm halal status directly with each venue.)

Nicolson Street & Square

A short walk from George Square: kebabs, curries, grills and late-night bites loved by students.

Newington & Southside

Cosy cafes and restaurants down the South Clerk Street stretch, perfect for a relaxed group meal.

Around Potterrow

Grab a quick halal lunch near the mosque and central campus before or after prayer.
