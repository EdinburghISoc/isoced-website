The Muslim Student Planner

نَظِّمْ دِينَكَ وَدُنْيَاكَ

Organise your deen, your studies, and your life.

Our flagship product

Designed for the student who wants it all in balance

A premium A5 planner of 193 pages, beautifully designed and Islamically-grounded, with almost 6,000 interactive fields: tappable prayer and Qur'an trackers, typeable notes, and a clickable contents page. The Muslim Student Planner helps you keep your worship, your wellbeing and your degree moving forward together, all in one place you'll actually want to open.

Inside the planner

What’s inside

Every section is crafted to nourish both your deen and your day-to-day student life.

Opening & Identity

A warm welcome, intentions for the year, and space to reflect on who you want to become.

Islamic Essentials

Key du’as, reminders and references to keep the fundamentals of your deen close at hand.

Monthly Planners

Month-at-a-glance spreads with the Hijri calendar alongside the Gregorian months.

Weekly Spreads

Plan lectures, deadlines and worship side by side, with room for daily prayers and goals.

Ramadan Planner

A dedicated section to make the most of the blessed month: fasting, Qur’an and good deeds.

Development Trackers

Prayer, Qur’an, habit and reflection trackers to help you grow consistently all year.
